## **EcoLoop: The Circular Trade Platform**

**EcoLoop** solves the "double coincidence of wants" by using an automated matching engine to create multi-person trade circles. It allows users to swap goods locally without the need for money.

---

### **The Core Innovation: Indirect Swaps**
Instead of searching for a direct 1:1 trade, EcoLoop identifies **"Perfect Circles."** > **Example:** **Riya** (Jacket) $\rightarrow$ **Arjun** (Books) $\rightarrow$ **Kabir** (Lamp) $\rightarrow$ **Riya**.

---

### **The 8-Step Journey**
1. **List & Request:** Post what you HAVE and what you WANT.
2. **Match & Notify:** The engine finds a local circle and alerts participants.
3. **Confirm & Connect:** Users accept the trade and receive contact details.
4. **Exchange & Complete:** Meet physically to swap, then earn **Karma** points.

---

### **Exchange Scenarios**

| Type | Scenario | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direct** | **1:1 Swap** | Simple two-way exchange. |
| **Circular**| **3-Way Circle** | The flagship multi-user trade engine. |
| **Social** | **NGO Donate** | One-way donation for maximum Karma. |

---

**Why EcoLoop?** By automating complex swaps and rewarding users with Karma, EcoLoop turns waste into a community resource.
